The Flaw with Single "All-in-One" AI Agents

If you feed a 300-page 10-K report into a single LLM prompt and ask it to do math, plan tasks, search the web, and write a memo all at once:

  • Context Window Dilution: The LLM loses track of early instructions.
  • Arithmetic Hallucinations: LLMs are language models; they miscalculate complex formulas when drowning in text.
  • Token Waste: Storing hundreds of raw database rows bloats every single subsequent prompt iteration.
The DeepAudit-AI Solution: Context Isolation

By delegating tasks to subagents running in isolated private scratchpads:

  • Deterministic Math: Calculations are computed by verified Python code, not guessed by an LLM.
  • Clean Executive Memory: The Lead Orchestrator only receives a crisp 3-4 bullet synthesis. Raw tables are discarded.
  • Unshakable Focus: The Orchestrator stays at ~2,000 tokens, maintaining perfect reasoning and clarity.

Forensic Accounting Formulas & Analytical Benchmarks

Every corporate analyst must know these quantitative screening tests. Here is how our subagents evaluate them automatically.

SubAgent 1 • Solvency Analysis

Altman Z-Score Solvency Metric

Edward Altman (1968)
Z = 1.2(X₁) + 1.4(X₂) + 3.3(X₃) + 0.6(X₄) + 0.999(X₅)
X₁:
Working Capital / Total Assets: Measures short-term liquidity cushion against unexpected drawdowns.
X₂:
Retained Earnings / Total Assets: Measures cumulative historical profitability vs debt reliance.
X₃:
EBIT / Total Assets: Pure productivity of assets independent of tax or leverage variations.
X₄:
Market Cap / Total Liabilities: How much company value can decline before liabilities exceed assets.
X₅:
Total Revenue / Total Assets: Asset turnover capability and revenue-generating efficiency.
Z > 2.99
SAFE ZONE
Minimal bankruptcy risk
1.81 – 2.99
GREY ZONE
Requires close monitoring
Z < 1.81
DISTRESS ZONE
High probability of default
SubAgent 2 • Forensics

Sloan Accrual Anomaly Ratio

Richard Sloan (1996)
Accrual Ratio = [(Net Income - Operating Cash Flow) / Avg Assets] × 100%

Why It Matters to Investors: A company can report record "Net Income" on paper while burning cash. If net income rises but operating cash flows decline, earnings are distorted by non-cash receivables or deferred expenses.

Ratio < -3.0%: High Cash Quality (Cash outpaces income)
-3.0% to +8.0%: Normal Seasonal Working Capital
Ratio > +10.0%: Dangerous Accrual Anomaly (Paper Profits)
