Step 1: The Listener (Browser Microphone & WebAudio)

How your voice is captured and prepared for the AI

What happens here?

When you press the microphone button, your browser's microphone captures the vibrations of your voice in the air. It converts those physical sound waves into digital numbers (called PCM audio samples).

  • Downsamples audio from 48,000 Hz to 16,000 Hz for optimal AI speech recognition.
  • Applies Noise Cancellation and Echo Suppression so only your clear voice is sent.
  • Sends live audio chunks in small 100-millisecond packages over a WebSocket connection.

💡 Simple Analogy for Students

Think of this like speaking into a walkie-talkie. Your voice is turned into an electric radio signal and sent over a wire to the sandwich shop.

Key Code Concept

// Capture mic & downsample to 16kHz
const pcm16 = downsampleBuffer(
    inputData, 
    audioContext.sampleRate, 
    16000
);
// Stream binary bytes to server
websocket.send(pcm16.buffer);

Why is it called the "Sandwich Architecture"?

Just like a sandwich has bread on top and bottom with the filling in the middle, our voice agent has speech on both ends with intelligent thinking in between!

🥖 Top Bread: Speech-to-Text (AssemblyAI) → 🥩 Filling: LangChain Agent (SenseNova AI + Tools) → 🥖 Bottom Bread: Text-to-Speech (Cartesia)
